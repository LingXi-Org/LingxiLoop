Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

LingxiLoop ("we", "us", "our") provides a workspace for people and AI teammates to collaborate. This policy explains what data the LingxiLoop Web app at loop.lingxilearn.cn collects, how we use it, and what choices you have. We collect only the data we need to make the product work and we never sell it.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign in we receive your email address and display name from the third-party provider you chose (Google, GitHub, or Lingxi). We store: your email, name, an avatar URL from the provider, and a unique provider ID so we can recognize your account when you sign in again.

Content you create. Messages, documents, task boards, calendar events, AI teammate settings, and reactions are stored on our servers so that you and your workspace teammates can see them. This content is scoped to the workspace you create it in; it is not visible to users outside that workspace and it is not used to train any model.

Service records. We keep basic connection details, including timestamps, IP addresses, browser details, and error information, to protect the service and investigate problems. We retain these records for up to 30 days. They are not used for advertising or sold.

Usage analytics. The app sends usage events (pages opened, features used) to PostHog so we can understand what to improve. These events are tied to your account, never include message or document content, and are not used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera roll, or microphone unless you explicitly share an attachment.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your messages or documents to train AI models.

Who we share data with

The data above is shared only with the infrastructure providers we need to operate the service:

  • Production infrastructure providers — application and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, attachment storage and delivery, and inbound email routing.
  • Resend — sending email on behalf of your workspace's agents.
  • PostHog — product analytics (usage events as described above).
  • Sign-in providers (Google, GitHub, Lingxi) — verifying your account.
  • AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, and others) — when an AI teammate responds. Workspace owners can configure the provider accounts used by their team.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose data only when legally compelled and only the specific records required.

Your rights

Account deletion. You can permanently delete your LingxiLoop account from your account settings in the Web app. Deletion immediately invalidates your sign-in sessions, removes your links to sign-in providers, clears your name, email, and avatar from our records, and removes your membership from every workspace. Your messages and other shared content remain, with your name replaced by a generic author label so your team's history stays intact.

Access and correction. You can view and edit your name and avatar in your account settings. For copies of audit logs or a data export, use the contact link below.

Children

LingxiLoop is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top whenever we do. Material changes will be announced inside the app before they take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the GitHub security page.
